Japanese lesson review

カードが使えないんですが...

Session date: 2026-07-09. Level: beginner to lower-intermediate. Focus: restaurant and shop payment trouble, cash-only notices, soft checking phrases, and calm staff-facing Japanese.

daily life restaurants cashless payment soft uncertainty

Topic and context

This lesson came from a current Japan-side discussion on July 9, 2026: after the bankruptcy of the payment processor 全東信（ぜんとうしん）, some restaurants and shops had card terminals become unusable and temporarily switched to 現金のみ.

For real life in Japan, this is useful because you may need to ask what payment methods work, explain that you do not have cash, or understand a staff member's polite explanation at the register.

Culture note

When payment trouble happens, Japanese staff often apologize even when the problem is not personally their fault. As a customer, using すみません, 念のため, and 確認したいんですが keeps your question calm and cooperative.

Grammar and patterns

1. 〜は使えますか: asking if something can be used

カードは使えますか。
Can I use a card?

交通系ICは使えますか。
Can I use transit IC?

2. 〜以外だと、何が使えますか: asking for alternatives

現金以外だと、何が使えますか。
Besides cash, what can I use?

3. 〜ていなくて...: soft reason, leaving space

今、現金を持っていなくて...
I do not have cash with me right now...

This ending is useful because it explains your situation without sounding like a demand.

4. 念のため確認したいんですが: calm checking

念のため確認したいんですが、カードは使えますか。
I would like to check just in case: can I use a card?

Useful phrases and contrast sets

Too blunt

カード使えない？

Card does not work?

This is understandable, but it can sound abrupt at a register.

Natural customer version

すみません、カードは今使えますか。

Excuse me, can I use a card right now?

Careful and soft

念のため確認したいんですが、カードは使えますか。

I would like to check just in case: can I use a card?

Customer phrases
  • カードは使えますか。 - Can I use a card?
  • 現金以外だと、何が使えますか。 - Besides cash, what can I use?
  • 交通系ICかPayPayは使えますか。 - Can I use transit IC or PayPay?
  • すみません、今現金を持っていなくて... - Sorry, I do not have cash right now...
  • 近くにATMはありますか。 - Is there an ATM nearby?
Staff-style polite phrases
  • 申し訳ございません。ただいま現金のみとなっております。 - We are sorry. At the moment, it is cash only.
  • 決済端末の不具合で、カードが使えない状況です。 - Due to an issue with the payment terminal, cards cannot be used.
  • ご不便をおかけして申し訳ございません。 - We apologize for the inconvenience.

Role-play and dialogue

Scenario: You are paying at a small restaurant. There is a handwritten sign near the register:

本日、カード決済がご利用いただけません。現金のみでお願いいたします。

Study this cleaned-up mock conversation. It expands the role-play prompt into a realistic register exchange.

店員
お会計、1,480円です。申し訳ありませんが、本日は現金のみとなっております。
Your total is 1,480 yen. We are sorry, but today it is cash only.
客
すみません、今現金を持っていなくて...。近くにATMはありますか。
Sorry, I do not have cash with me right now... Is there an ATM nearby?
店員
はい。この先のコンビニにATMがあります。
Yes. There is an ATM at the convenience store just ahead.
客
ありがとうございます。念のため確認したいんですが、交通系ICも使えませんか。
Thank you. I would like to check just in case: transit IC cannot be used either?
店員
申し訳ございません。決済端末の不具合で、今は現金のみです。
We are very sorry. Because of an issue with the payment terminal, it is cash only for now.
客
分かりました。すぐ下ろしてきます。
Understood. I will go withdraw cash right away.

My answers and corrections

No learner answer was submitted yet in the live role-play. The target response for the prompt was:

This sounds natural because it gives a soft reason first, then asks for a practical next step.

Mistake review

  • Too direct: Avoid starting with only カード使えない？ at a register. Add すみません and use 使えますか.
  • Register mismatch: Staff-facing explanations often use 申し訳ございません and 〜となっております. As a customer, you usually do not need to be that formal.
  • Missing nuance: 念のため makes a repeated or careful question sound cooperative, not suspicious.

Cultural notes

  • 現金のみ may appear on handwritten signs during temporary payment trouble.
  • ご利用いただけません is a common polite notice phrase meaning "cannot be used."
  • When you need an alternative, 現金以外だと、何が使えますか is practical and not confrontational.
  • If staff apologize, a simple 分かりました or 大丈夫です keeps the interaction smooth.
